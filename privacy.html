Privacy policy

CallOps requests read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) for the signed-in account only.

  • Calendar data is used solely to show meeting reminders to the account owner on the owner's own computer.
  • Data is processed locally. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored locally in the operating system's secure keychain.
  • CallOps never creates, changes or deletes calendar events.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
